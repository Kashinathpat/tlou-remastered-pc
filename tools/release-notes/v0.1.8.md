**The Last of Us Remastered: PC port** 0.1.8

Fixes the Pittsburgh crash on GTX 900/10 graphics cards, adds raw mouse look for the camera and the
aim, and the launcher now updates the port by itself.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own decrypted copy of the game and its update. This
> project is not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment or Naughty Dog.

## Fixes and improvements

- **Fixed the Vulkan device loss in Pittsburgh on NVIDIA GTX 900 and 10 series cards** (once Henry
  and Sam appear; the game stopped with `Device lost during submit`). The cause was the game's cloth
  solver for its largest cloths, which needs every register of a GPU multiprocessor; on these cards
  it now runs as 256 threads that each simulate 2 vertices, with the same math. A replay of that
  route lost the device in 17 of 18 runs before, and in none of 6 after. Newer cards are not
  affected and run it as before. The option is in the launcher's **Debug → At start** tab
  ("Cloth workaround on GTX 900/10").
- **Draw pipeline on again.** It was blamed for that crash, but only made it more frequent; the
  faster two-stage draw pipeline is back on by default.
- **Raw mouse look.** In gameplay the mouse now turns the third-person camera and the aim directly,
  with no stick curve, dead zone, acceleration or top speed. Mouse sensitivity, aim sensitivity and
  the invert options apply. Climbing, vehicle and scripted cameras still take the mouse as the
  right stick.
- **Automatic updates.** Each time it opens, the launcher looks for a newer release on GitHub. When
  there is one, an **Update to x.y.z** button appears beside **Play**: it downloads the package,
  checks it against the SHA-256 that GitHub publishes, installs it and opens the launcher again.
  Saves, settings, logs and the shader cache (the `analysis` folder) are never touched. If anything
  fails, the previous version is restored. Adapted from BilalReloaded's Uncharted port.
- **More accurate GPU compute shaders**, ported from other PS4 ports: shared-memory reads past the
  end return 0 as on the PS4, large compute workgroups that share nothing run in smaller groups,
  and draws whose inputs changed while being prepared, or texture sets read during an image
  change, are no longer reused.

## Update

**From 0.1.7 or older:** extract the zip over your previous folder (versions before 0.1.8 have no
updater). Your saves, settings and logs are in its `analysis` folder: if you use a new folder,
copy `analysis` across first. **From 0.1.8 on,** use the launcher's **Update** button.

The first run after updating compiles every compute shader again, once (their cache format
changed), so expect more short pauses than usual at first.

## New install

1. Download **TLOU-Remastered-windows.zip** below and extract it to a folder with plenty of free space.
2. Open **TLOU Launcher.exe**.
3. Click **Folders** and choose **Game files**: the folder with `eboot.bin`. Update 1.11 is looked
   for beside it, in the folder of the same name ending in `-patch`.
4. Click **Play**. You can also open **TLOU Game.exe** to start with your saved launcher settings.

## Known issues

- **Shots pass through some parts of objects** with no effect, though they hit walls and most
  objects where the crosshair is. One firefly pendant, hanging from a tree in the courtyard after
  the quarantine zone, cannot be shot down for that reason.
- **Intel graphics cards do not work yet.** The game crashes at the start logos or the main menu,
  and the scene behind the menu is white.
- Soft shadows can look detached from objects, with a thin light outline around them and little
  darkening where they touch the ground, most visibly under vehicle wheels.
- NPCs sometimes move in jumps. It comes and goes between runs; a fixed 30 FPS limit had no jumps
  in the sessions tried, but this is not a general fix for unstable frame rates.
- Short pauses can occur the first time an area is played while its shaders compile.
- DLSS and FSR 4 are not in this package: DLSS falls back to FSR 3.

When reporting a problem, attach `analysis\tlou\boot.log` (**Open log** in the launcher).
