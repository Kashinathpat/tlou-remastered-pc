**The Last of Us Remastered: PC port** 0.1.9

First Intel graphics support, ultrawide monitors, the launcher's Resolution now sets the game's own
render size, older Intel CPUs (Sandy and Ivy Bridge) and a walk key.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own decrypted copy of the game and its update. This
> project is not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment or Naughty Dog.

## Fixes and improvements

- **Intel graphics.** On an Intel UHD 630 the menu came out white or with magenta textures and the
  game lost the Vulkan device when a save loaded. It now plays: 3 runs of 5 minutes of play with no
  crash (all 3 crashed before), about 10 FPS at 1080p on that chip. The causes, found on the UHD
  630 and changed for Intel's driver only:
  - the game's shaders ask for float rounding toward zero, which Intel's driver applies differently
    from NVIDIA's: that made the menu white. It is no longer used there;
  - a compute shader has GPU barriers that only some of its threads reach; the PS4 and NVIDIA
    tolerate it, Intel's driver waited forever. Those parts now run for every thread;
  - large textures were converted in more work groups than Intel allows, and image copies with
    mipmaps stopped the game on GPUs without `VK_KHR_maintenance8`: both fixed;
  - after the God of War III port (SpartanPort): vertex input dynamic state off, shared-memory
    barriers like NVIDIA's, a fixed compute subgroup size, and no per-sample shading on
    single-sample draws.

  **Arc cards (A and B series) are not tested with this version yet.** Reports are welcome.
- **Ultrawide monitors (21:9, 32:9).** A Resolution of another aspect ratio than 16:9 now shows
  more at the sides with the same vertical view, keeps the interface 16:9 in the middle with its
  crosshair, icons and collectibles preview in place, and plays the movies with black bars. The
  launcher lists 2560x1080, 3440x1440, 3840x1600, 3840x1080 and 5120x1440. Patches after
  SanyaGames227's shadPS4 ultrawide mod.
- **The launcher's Resolution sets the game's render size.** The game always drew at 1920x1080
  before; it now renders at the size you choose, up to 4K (the game's memory grows as on the PS4
  Pro). The launcher adds 2880x1620 and 3200x1800. On a GTX 1050 Ti Mobile: 720p at 60 FPS,
  1440p about 34 FPS, 4K about 15 FPS.
- **Intel Core 2nd and 3rd generation (Sandy and Ivy Bridge) CPUs.** The game's instructions those
  CPUs lack (BMI1, LZCNT, MOVBE, F16C) are replaced by equivalent code when the game starts, after
  vitolasanha's Sandy Bridge fork. About 10% fewer FPS in the busiest areas than on a newer CPU.
  Their integrated graphics have no Vulkan: a graphics card is still needed.
- **Walk key.** Left Alt walks at half speed (adjustable), held or toggled; set in the launcher's
  Controls window or the in-game menu.
- Frame captures no longer swap red and blue on some graphics cards.
- **TLOU Game.exe** no longer stops with an error when started without a console, and the starters'
  error messages follow the launcher's language.

## Update

**From 0.1.8:** use the launcher's **Update** button. **From 0.1.7 or older:** extract the zip
over your previous folder. Your saves, settings and logs are in its `analysis` folder.

On Intel graphics, the first run compiles every shader again, once, so expect pauses at first.

## New install

1. Download **TLOU-Remastered-windows.zip** below and extract it to a folder with plenty of free space.
2. Open **TLOU Launcher.exe**.
3. Click **Folders** and choose **Game files**: the folder with `eboot.bin`. Update 1.11 is looked
   for beside it, in the folder of the same name ending in `-patch`.
4. Click **Play**. You can also open **TLOU Game.exe** to start with your saved launcher settings.

Intel integrated graphics (6th generation and newer) need Intel's own current driver for Vulkan
1.3; a laptop maker's older driver can stop at Vulkan 1.2.

## Known issues

- **Intel Arc cards** are untested with these fixes, and Intel integrated graphics are slow (about
  10 FPS at 1080p on a UHD 630).
- **Shots pass through some parts of objects** with no effect, though they hit walls and most
  objects where the crosshair is. One firefly pendant, hanging from a tree in the courtyard after
  the quarantine zone, cannot be shot down for that reason.
- Soft shadows can look detached from objects, with a thin light outline around them and little
  darkening where they touch the ground, most visibly under vehicle wheels.
- NPCs sometimes move in jumps. It comes and goes between runs; a fixed 30 FPS limit had no jumps
  in the sessions tried, but this is not a general fix for unstable frame rates.
- Short pauses can occur the first time an area is played while its shaders compile.
- DLSS and FSR 4 are not in this package: DLSS falls back to FSR 3.

When reporting a problem, attach `analysis\tlou\boot.log` (**Open log** in the launcher).
