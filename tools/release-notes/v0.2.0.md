**The Last of Us Remastered: PC port** 0.2.0

NPCs no longer move in jumps, trophies are saved and shown, the controller's vibration can be
turned off, and a clear message for game folders that mix the base game and the update.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own decrypted copy of the game and its update. This
> project is not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment or Naughty Dog.

## Fixes and improvements

- **NPCs no longer move in jumps** ([#57](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/57),
  [#63](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/63)). Enemies and companions shook,
  bobbed up and down or sank into the floor, at the hydroelectric plant, in Bill's Town and in
  Pittsburgh. The game reads some results of its GPU memory copy shader, and the port gave it those
  results a frame late. They now reach the game in time. On a save where NPCs jumped every time,
  they stopped, with no FPS cost. Thanks to KyrieFrost, whose experimental patch in #63 pointed at
  that shader.

  This was tested on one save and one graphics card so far: if NPCs still jump somewhere, please
  report it with your log. The fix can be turned off for comparison in the launcher's **Debug** tab
  (**At start** > **GPU copy readback (NPCs jumping)**).
- **Cloth on every character is up to date.** The cloth simulation's results reached the game in
  time only for one of its four sizes; all four do now (**Debug** > **At start** > **Cloth joints
  for every cloth size**).
- **Trophies are saved and shown.** Every unlocked trophy was dropped before: nothing was saved, no
  popup came up and the menu's Trophies tab stayed empty. Trophies earned from now on are kept.
- **Controller vibration can be turned off**, in the launcher's **Controls** window or the in-game
  menu's Controls tab. The Controls window opens on a new **Controller** tab that shows the connected
  controller's buttons, triggers and sticks live, with a button to test the vibration.
- **Game folders with the update merged in.** If update 1.11 was copied into the game folder but
  the base game's `eboot.bin` stayed, the game stopped at start with an unsupported import
  (`libSceRemoteplay`). **Play** now says what is wrong and how to fix it: copy the update's
  `eboot.bin`, `sce_module` and `sce_sys` over the game's, or keep the update in its own folder
  beside the game, ending in `-patch`.
- **Intel Arc cards have been tested:** the game runs well on them, with no crashes in the session
  played.
- The launcher's Debug tab is now fully translated in all its languages.

## Update

**From 0.1.8 or 0.1.9:** use the launcher's **Update** button. **From 0.1.7 or older:** extract the
zip over your previous folder. Your saves, settings and logs are in its `analysis` folder.

## New install

1. Download **TLOU-Remastered-windows.zip** below and extract it to a folder with plenty of free space.
2. Open **TLOU Launcher.exe**.
3. Click **Folders** and choose **Game files**: the folder with `eboot.bin`. Update 1.11 is looked
   for beside it, in the folder of the same name ending in `-patch`.
4. Click **Play**. You can also open **TLOU Game.exe** to start with your saved launcher settings.

Intel integrated graphics (6th generation and newer) need Intel's own current driver for Vulkan
1.3; a laptop maker's older driver can stop at Vulkan 1.2.

## Known issues

- Intel integrated graphics are slow (about 10 FPS at 1080p on a UHD 630).
- **Shots pass through some parts of objects** with no effect, though they hit walls and most
  objects where the crosshair is. One firefly pendant, hanging from a tree in the courtyard after
  the quarantine zone, cannot be shot down for that reason.
- Soft shadows can look detached from objects, with a thin light outline around them and little
  darkening where they touch the ground, most visibly under vehicle wheels.
- Short pauses can occur the first time an area is played while its shaders compile.
- DLSS and FSR 4 are not in this package: DLSS falls back to FSR 3.

When reporting a problem, attach `analysis\tlou\boot.log` (**Open log** in the launcher).
