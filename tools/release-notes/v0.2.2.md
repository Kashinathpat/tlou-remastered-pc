**The Last of Us Remastered: PC port** 0.2.2

Soft shadows stay attached to characters and vehicles at any resolution, the keyboard has a new
default layout, and the launcher explains its options.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own decrypted copy of the game and its update. This
> project is not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment or Naughty Dog.

## Fixes and improvements

- **Soft shadows under characters and vehicles** ([#5](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/5),
  [#66](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/66)). Above 1080p, the soft
  shadows of characters and vehicles were drawn away from them and missing under feet and wheels,
  with a light or dark outline around them. The game adjusts these shaders to the render size when
  it loads them, but the port's shader cache kept the 1080p versions. Shaders cached for another
  resolution now compile again once, so the first start after updating can pause a little more
  while they do. At 1080p, the thin light band along the edges of characters is also gone.
- **Two new options in the launcher's Video section**, both on by default:
  - **Contact shadows at half resolution**: the game computes these shadows at a quarter of the
    render size, which leaves a light ring where wheels and feet touch the ground. At half size
    the ring is half as wide, at some cost in frame rate: turn it off if you need the frames.
  - **Align contact shadows with object edges**: removes the outline described above, with no
    noticeable cost.
- **New default keyboard and mouse layout**: WASD or the arrow keys move, the mouse turns the
  camera, right and left mouse buttons aim and fire, **Space** or **Enter** Cross, **C** Circle,
  **F** Square, **E** Triangle, **Left Shift** L1, **Tab** R1, **V** L3, **T** R3, **Esc** or
  **Backspace** Options, **Caps Lock** the touchpad (backpack, crafting), **1 2 3 4** and the mouse
  wheel the d-pad, **G** shake, and **Left Ctrl** walks (now 75% of a full push, was 50%). If you
  saved your keys in the launcher's **Controls** window, they are kept; its **Restore defaults**
  switches to the new layout.
- **In-game Controls tab**: each row is named by the PS4 button it presses, in the launcher's
  order, with a row for shake, and keys are shown readably (Enter, Esc, Caps Lock, Up arrow).
- **Launcher**: an **(i)** beside each main-screen option explains what it does, in all nine
  languages.

## Update

**From 0.1.8 or newer:** use the launcher's **Update** button. **From 0.1.7 or older:** extract the
zip over your previous folder. Your saves, settings and logs are in its `analysis` folder.

## New install

1. Download **TLOU-Remastered-windows.zip** below and extract it to a folder with plenty of free space.
2. Open **TLOU Launcher.exe**.
3. Click **Folders** and choose **Game files**: the folder with `eboot.bin`. Update 1.11 is looked
   for beside it, in the folder of the same name ending in `-patch`.
4. Click **Play**. You can also open **TLOU Game.exe** to start with your saved launcher settings.

Intel integrated graphics (6th generation and newer) need Intel's own current driver for Vulkan
1.3; a laptop maker's older driver can stop at Vulkan 1.2.

## Known issues

- Intel integrated graphics are slow (about 10 FPS at 1080p on a UHD 630).
- A thin light ring can remain where wheels and feet touch the ground: the game's own design,
  made thinner by **Contact shadows at half resolution**.
- Short pauses can occur the first time an area is played while its shaders compile.
- DLSS and FSR 4 are not in this package: DLSS falls back to FSR 3.
- Online play (Factions) is not available.

When reporting a problem, attach `analysis\tlou\boot.log` (**Open log** in the launcher).
