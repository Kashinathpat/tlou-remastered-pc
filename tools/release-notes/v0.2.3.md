**The Last of Us Remastered: PC port** 0.2.3

All of the game's languages in its menus, a camera that follows the mouse at once, a much faster
shader cache warm-up, and ultrawide fixes.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own decrypted copy of the game and its update. This
> project is not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment or Naughty Dog.

**Discord:** the project now has an open Discord server for help, testing and news:
https://discord.gg/9mnjjezcNj

## Fixes and improvements

- **All 21 of the game's languages** ([#156](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/156)).
  The Americas release only offered English, French, Latin American Spanish and Brazilian
  Portuguese, but the text of every language is already in its files. The game's **Language**
  menu now offers, for text and subtitles: English, French, Italian, German, Spanish, Latin
  American Spanish, Portuguese, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch, Danish, Finnish, Norwegian, Swedish,
  Polish, Russian, Czech, Greek, Turkish and Japanese.
  - **Korean and Chinese** (traditional) appear when chosen as the launcher's new **Game
    language**, the language the game starts in. A language already chosen in the game's own
    menu is kept.
  - **Dialogue** offers the dubs whose files are in your game folder. German, Italian, Spanish
    (Spain), Portuguese (Portugal), Polish, Russian and Japanese voices need that dub's
    `<language>-sp.psarc` (and `<language>-mp.psarc` for multiplayer) from your copy of another
    region's release, copied to `build\ps4\main\sound1\streams\speech` in the game folder.
- **The camera follows the mouse at once** ([#138](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/138)).
  Outside aiming, the camera kept turning after the mouse stopped, like mouse smoothing. It now
  stops the frame after the mouse does; the game's own camera moves keep their easing.
- **Faster start**: the shader cache warm-up took about 95 seconds on a GTX 1050 Ti laptop and
  now takes about 20, by reading the cache from two pack files instead of some 40,000 small ones.
  The cache uses about 330 MB more disk space. The first start after updating builds the packs.
- **Fewer stutters from compute shaders** ([#151](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/151)):
  on NVIDIA and AMD graphics cards these are now kept on disk too, instead of being compiled
  again during play. Intel graphics and AMD integrated GPUs keep their driver's pipeline cache on
  disk.
- **Ultrawide** ([#139](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/139),
  [#143](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/143)): the target circle of bricks,
  bottles and bombs stays on the target instead of drifting to the screen's edge, and the menus'
  dimming, the fades, the damage effect and the rifle scope's black frame now cover the whole
  screen.
- **A crash after long play in Pittsburgh** ([#150](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/150)):
  a texture could ask for more of an image than it has, and the game stopped. The request is now
  cut to the image, and the log names where it came from if it happens again.

## Update

**From 0.1.8 or newer:** use the launcher's **Update** button. **From 0.1.7 or older:** extract the
zip over your previous folder. Your saves, settings and logs are in its `analysis` folder.

## New install

1. Download **TLOU-Remastered-windows.zip** below and extract it to a folder with plenty of free space.
2. Open **TLOU Launcher.exe**.
3. Click **Folders** and choose **Game files**: the folder with `eboot.bin`. Update 1.11 is looked
   for beside it, in the folder of the same name ending in `-patch`.
4. Click **Play**. You can also open **TLOU Game.exe** to start with your saved launcher settings.

Intel integrated graphics (6th generation and newer) need Intel's own current driver for Vulkan
1.3; a laptop maker's older driver can stop at Vulkan 1.2.

## Known issues

- Intel integrated graphics are slow (about 10 FPS at 1080p on a UHD 630).
- A thin light ring can remain where wheels and feet touch the ground: the game's own design,
  made thinner by **Contact shadows at half resolution**.
- Short pauses can occur the first time an area is played while its shaders compile.
- DLSS and FSR 4 are not in this package: DLSS falls back to FSR 3.
- Dubs from other regions have not been tried with files from a European or Japanese copy yet.
- Online play (Factions) is not available.

When reporting a problem, attach `analysis\tlou\boot.log` (**Open log** in the launcher), or ask
on the Discord server.
