**The Last of Us Remastered: PC port** 0.2.1

Shots now hit what the crosshair is on, aim assist can be turned off, and the mouse looks up and
down as fast as it turns.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own decrypted copy of the game and its update. This
> project is not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment or Naughty Dog.

## Fixes and improvements

- **Shots hit what the crosshair is on** ([#29](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/29),
  [#45](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/45)). Pistol and rifle shots went
  through walls and objects with no bullet hole or effect, while a shotgun marked the same walls,
  and some firefly pendants could not be shot down. There were two causes, both fixed:
  - The game asks the graphics card whether a character is under the crosshair, and while one is,
    its bullets go through thin cover in front of the target. The port always answered "yes", so
    bullets ignored all scenery. The graphics card now answers for real.
  - The results of the game's bullet ray casts, computed on the graphics card, reached the game
    too late, so a shot could take the hit of an earlier, unrelated ray. They now arrive in time.

  Walls take bullet holes again and the pendant in the courtyard after the quarantine zone falls.
- **Aim assist can be turned off** ([#123](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/123),
  [#127](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/127)).
  The launcher's **Controls** window has an **Aim assist** switch, in the **Controller** tab
  (**Aiming** card) and in the **Mouse** tab: the same setting for both. Off, every weapon keeps its
  speeds, zoom and stick curves, without the pull towards targets. It is on by default, and
  **Restore defaults** turns it back on.
- **Mouse** ([#123](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/123)):
  - Looking up and down without aiming was slow: the camera now moves up and down as many degrees
    per mouse movement as it turns left and right, as it already did while aiming. **Mouse up/down
    speed**, in the in-game menu's Controls tab, still scales it.
  - The camera while pushing a crate, and other cameras the mouse does not drive directly, did not
    turn with the mouse. The mouse moves them again, as the right stick does.

## Update

**From 0.1.8 or newer:** use the launcher's **Update** button. **From 0.1.7 or older:** extract the
zip over your previous folder. Your saves, settings and logs are in its `analysis` folder.

## New install

1. Download **TLOU-Remastered-windows.zip** below and extract it to a folder with plenty of free space.
2. Open **TLOU Launcher.exe**.
3. Click **Folders** and choose **Game files**: the folder with `eboot.bin`. Update 1.11 is looked
   for beside it, in the folder of the same name ending in `-patch`.
4. Click **Play**. You can also open **TLOU Game.exe** to start with your saved launcher settings.

Intel integrated graphics (6th generation and newer) need Intel's own current driver for Vulkan
1.3; a laptop maker's older driver can stop at Vulkan 1.2.

## Known issues

- Intel integrated graphics are slow (about 10 FPS at 1080p on a UHD 630).
- Soft shadows can look detached from objects, with a thin light outline around them and little
  darkening where they touch the ground, most visibly under vehicle wheels.
- Short pauses can occur the first time an area is played while its shaders compile.
- DLSS and FSR 4 are not in this package: DLSS falls back to FSR 3.

When reporting a problem, attach `analysis\tlou\boot.log` (**Open log** in the launcher).
